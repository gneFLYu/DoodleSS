CHART KEY

Published glyph and line semantics

The canvas follows DKLLW Tables 10–11. Page fate controls visibility and arrows; it does not recolor a module glyph, and colours do not encode algebraic order. E2 has exactly two presentation families: oriented (the integer pattern) and non-oriented (the sigmai pattern); I/S codes are internal module slots, not additional E2 patterns.

DKLLW dot / fat dot / circle / square; completed-series refinement — dot: k; filled ring: F4[[j]]; hollow ring: a truncated j-adic family whose h1-tower loses one bottom j-grading every 4 stems; double square: the W(F4)[[j]] 2-adic tower. Scalar levels such as 2D and 4D live inside that square and are not extra dots. Hover a hollow ring for its current lower j-order. These are coefficient patterns, independent of fate.
Multiplication lines — vertical: 2 in the same cell; slope 1: h1; slope 1/3: h2; dashed in 2BSS: hidden extension.
Solid charcoal arrow — accepted differential claim.
Amber dashed arrow — candidate or under-review differential.
Atlas coefficients — point labels retain transported scalars. Known incoming combinations can form an adapted display basis; arrow and relation coefficients then use that displayed basis. Coefficient 1 is omitted; ? means unresolved. Σ denotes an exact dependent combination, not an extra basis generator: select it to inspect its expression and coordinates. Stored equations and their computational basis are unchanged.
